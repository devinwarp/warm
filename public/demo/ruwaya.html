03 Booking

How to book, and what happens if you can't make it

Appointments are taken by phone or WhatsApp on the number below. Colour and keratin services need a deposit of AED 100, which comes off the final bill.

Move or cancel with at least 48 hours' notice and the deposit carries over to the new date. Less than 48 hours and we keep it — the chair usually can't be refilled that late.

Blow-dries are walk-in if a chair is free. Everything else is by appointment.
