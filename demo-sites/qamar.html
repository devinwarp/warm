The room

Forty covers downstairs, eighteen on the terrace. The terrace is closed between June and September because nobody wants to sit on it.

Hours & booking

We hold a third of the room back for walk-ins every night, so it is always worth turning up.

Reservations

Tables of one to four are walk-in only — we don't take bookings for them. Five or more can be reserved by phone, and we hold the table for fifteen minutes past the booked time. Groups of ten and over are asked for a set menu, agreed in advance.
