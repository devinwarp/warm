General dentistry, hygiene and Invisalign for adults and children over six. We are a small practice and we keep the same dentist with you from the first examination onward.

01 — Treatments

What we treat

We do not publish treatment prices, and we would rather explain why than leave you guessing. The same treatment can differ by several thousand dirhams depending on what an X-ray shows, and a figure quoted over the phone would be a number we could not honour.

Every patient gets a written treatment plan with itemised costs after the first examination. Nothing is started before you have signed it. If you would like an estimate before booking, the practice manager will call you back once she has seen your X-rays.

01

Examination and X-rays

45 minutes Quoted after exam

Full mouth assessment, two bitewing X-rays, and a written plan. New patients are always given the longer slot.

02

Hygiene, scale and polish

30 minutes Quoted after exam

With one of two hygienists. Deep cleaning below the gum line is a separate, longer appointment.

03

Composite filling

30–60 minutes Quoted after exam

Tooth-coloured, placed in a single visit. Cost depends on the number of surfaces involved.

04

Root canal treatment

Two visits Quoted after exam

Front teeth and molars are very different pieces of work, which is the clearest example of why we don't publish a figure.

05

Crowns and onlays

Two visits Quoted after exam

Milled by a laboratory in Dubai; usually two weeks between the preparation and the fit.

06

Invisalign

6–18 months Quoted after scan

Includes a digital scan, the aligners, and every review appointment until the case is finished.

07

Teeth whitening, take-home

Two appointments Quoted after exam

Custom trays and gel. We don't offer in-chair laser whitening.

08

Children's check-up

30 minutes Quoted after exam

From age six. Younger children are referred to a paediatric specialist we work with in Al Safa.

03 — Appointments

Booking and cancellations

Appointments are made by phone during opening hours. New patients are booked into a 45-minute first visit, so please say it is your first time when you call.

We ask for 24 hours' notice to change or cancel. A missed appointment without notice is charged at the value of the reserved chair time, and that figure is on your treatment plan before you agree to it.

Insurance: we are in network with several of the major UAE insurers, but the list changes and cover varies by plan. Please call with your card details and the practice manager will confirm before you attend.
